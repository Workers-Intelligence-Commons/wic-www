Workers' Intelligence Commons now has a site. It says what the project is for, what is being built, how to help, and where the money will go once there is any.

## Where things stand

The project is run by one person. The association is not founded yet: its statutes and constitution exist as drafts, summed up on the [governance](/en/governance/) page, and they still have to be reviewed by a lawyer.

No donation channel is open. No money goes through a personal account, so donations will wait until a fiscal host or the association can receive them. The [transparency](/en/transparency/) page starts at zero and will stay up to date.

No WPT checkpoint has been published. The [repository](https://github.com/Workers-Intelligence-Commons/wpt) holds the code that builds the corpus, trains and evaluates the model.

## What helps now

Compute grants, and contributions to the code, the research and the review of sources. The [support](/en/support/) page explains how.

The site itself is free software. Its source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Workers-Intelligence-Commons/wic-www), and corrections are welcome.
